# Evidence Rules

Every major claim should follow:

CLAIM
↓
EVIDENCE
↓
SOURCE
↓
VERIFICATION
↓
CONFIDENCE

---

## Claim categories

FACT

EMPIRICAL_FINDING

THEORETICAL_CLAIM

INTERPRETATION

INFERENCE

OPINION

HYPOTHESIS

---

## Evidence strength

STRONG:

Multiple high-quality independent sources support the claim.

MODERATE:

At least one credible source supports the claim,
but evidence is limited.

WEAK:

Limited or indirect evidence.

CONFLICTING:

Credible sources reach different conclusions.

INSUFFICIENT:

Evidence is not adequate.

---

## Contradictory evidence

Actively search for evidence that could contradict
the proposed hypothesis.

Do not search only for confirming evidence.
