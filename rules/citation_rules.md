# Citation Rules

## Rule 1

Every important academic or factual claim should have evidence when appropriate.

## Rule 2

A citation must actually support the claim.

Do not cite a paper merely because its topic is similar.

## Rule 3

Distinguish:

Direct evidence
Indirect evidence
Background evidence
Contradictory evidence

## Rule 4

Do not invent DOI.

## Rule 5

Do not invent page numbers.

## Rule 6

Do not cite search snippets as if they were original evidence.

## Rule 7

When a paper cannot be verified:

mark it UNVERIFIED.

## Rule 8

When writing a literature review:

Do not merely list papers.

Compare:

- research questions
- data
- methods
- conclusions
- limitations
